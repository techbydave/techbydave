# 1. Fake Black Friday deals: the 10-second test

**Format:** on-camera Short (you, at your desk, phone screen recordings cut in), 45-55 seconds
**Post:** this week, ideally Wed Nov 25 or Thu Nov 26, before the Nov 27 deal drop
**Links to:** the blog post "Amazon Black Friday 2026: When the Best Deals Drop"
**Why it'll do well:** everyone is shopping this week, it's useful, and the "fake deal" angle gives people a reason to share it with family.

## Hook options (pick one, film all three if you can and test)

- "That Black Friday deal? It might be the same price it was in October."
- "Here's how I check if a Black Friday deal is fake in 10 seconds."
- "Amazon's biggest deals drop at 3 a.m. Here's what to do before then."

## Script

| Time | You say | On screen |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 0-3s | "That Black Friday deal might be the same price it was in October." | Text: **FAKE DEAL?** Quick zoom on a "deal" badge (screen recording) |
| 3-10s | "Amazon's Black Friday Week runs all the way to November 30th, so you've got time to check before you buy." | Text: **NOV 19 → NOV 30** |
| 10-25s | "Step one: open the price history. If the 'was' price only showed up a week ago, it's not a real discount. Step two: check the same thing at Best Buy and the maker's own store." | Screen recording: you opening a price-history chart. Arrow pointing at the spike before the "drop" |
| 25-38s | "And the biggest drops? Black Friday and Cyber Monday, at 3 a.m. and 11 a.m. Eastern. Midnight and 8 a.m. Pacific." | Text: **3 AM + 11 AM ET** with a clock graphic |
| 38-48s | "Small stuff like cables and Fire sticks? Fine to buy now. TVs and laptops? Wait for those drops." | Two columns: **BUY NOW** / **WAIT** with product icons |
| 48-55s | "Full list of times and what to skip is on techbydave.com. So... is that deal real?" | Verdict stamp: **WAIT** + logo. (The question loops back to the hook.) |

## Shot list

- A-roll: you at your desk, eye level, Blackmagic Camera app, 4K 30fps, ring/key light at 45°.
- Screen recordings (iPhone screen record): an Amazon deal page, a price-history chart, Best Buy's page for the same item.
- B-roll: close-up of a credit card going back in a wallet (no numbers visible), a clock showing 3:00, an alarm going off.
