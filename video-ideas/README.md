## What makes these work (use it on every video)

1. **First 1.5 seconds = the payoff in a sentence.** No "hey guys". Say the most surprising number or claim first, with it on screen in big text.
2. **A loop.** End on a line that leads straight back into the first line, so the Short replays.
3. **A change on screen every 2-3 seconds:** a cut, a zoom, a text pop, a new B-roll clip.
4. **Captions always on**, 2-4 words at a time, centred in the top two-thirds (the bottom third gets covered by the YouTube UI).
5. **One question in the pinned comment** to get replies ("Would you pay $899 for this?").
6. **Verdict stamp** (BUY / WAIT / SKIP) as the last frame, in the blog's colours, so every Short looks like TechByDave.
7. **Link the blog post** in the pinned comment and description; the post is the "full answer".
