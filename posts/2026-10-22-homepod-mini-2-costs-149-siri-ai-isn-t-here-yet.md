The [HomePod mini 2](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=HomePod+mini+2&tag=techtreck02-20) is a good small speaker that costs more than it used to, and the feature Apple built its launch around isn't on it yet. Buy it now if you want a compact Apple speaker for a desk, kitchen or Mac. If Siri AI is the reason you're buying, wait until it actually ships.

*Updated November 16, 2026.*

## HomePod mini 2: the quick verdict

**Wait, unless you need a small speaker now.** Apple's own product page lists Siri AI as "coming in a future software update," and Apple hasn't given a date. Independent reviews published in November say the same thing: the speaker doesn't have Siri AI yet. Everything else (sound, Wi-Fi, Mac support) works today, so the speaker is fine. The pitch just isn't finished.

## What's new in the HomePod mini 2

Apple announced the HomePod mini 2 on October 13, 2026. It's the first major update since the original HomePod mini arrived in November 2020, and it went on sale October 30 in the U.S. and more than 23 other countries and regions. It comes in pink, green, blue, white and midnight, with color-matched woven power cables.

The upgrades are mostly inside:

- **Chip:** the Apple S10, the chip from the Apple Watch Series 10 and Series 11, replaces the original's S5 from the 2019 Apple Watch Series 5. The S10 is the main reason the mini 2 can run Siri AI and the original mini can't.
- **Wireless:** Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 6, plus Thread and an Ultra Wideband chip. Bring a compatible iPhone close and music hands off to the speaker.
- **Mac speaker:** it can be the default audio output for Macs with Wi-Fi 6E or later, as one speaker or a stereo pair.
- **Home theater:** up to four HomePods can work as surround speakers with an Apple TV 4K.
- **Same speaker design:** it's still one full-range driver with dual passive radiators. Macworld says the biggest improvements are in software, audio and wireless, not the physical design.

It still works as a home hub, and it can listen for smoke or carbon monoxide alarms and send you an alert.

## HomePod mini 2 price and where to buy

The HomePod mini 2 costs **$149** in the U.S. and £129 in the UK. That's up from the original's $99 launch price, after Apple raised the old mini to $129 in June 2026 because of memory and storage costs.

- **Best Buy** sells it at $149, and Amazon wasn't carrying it at launch.
- **Costco** had it for **$145** in October, according to Gear Patrol. That's only $5 off, but it's the best price reported so far.
- **Black Friday** is November 27. Apple speakers rarely get big discounts, so don't expect much more than a few dollars off a brand-new model.

If you're building surround sound, the cost adds up fast. Four HomePod mini 2 speakers come to $596 before you add an [Apple TV 4K](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Apple+TV+4K&tag=techtreck02-20).

## Who should buy it, and who should skip it

## Buy it if
- You use a Mac with Wi-Fi 6E or later and want a small speaker on your desk. The Mac speaker feature is a real reason to pick this over a Bluetooth speaker.
- You want a compact speaker for a kitchen, bedroom or office and already use Apple Music and an iPhone.
- You're adding speakers to a home that already has HomePods.

## Wait if
- Siri AI is the main reason you're buying. When it does arrive, the owner of the home needs an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, and personal requests only work for people whose own iPhone or iPad supports it.
- You already own an original HomePod mini that works. The new one is better, but the original still plays music and runs your smart home.

## Skip it if
- You use Android. HomePods are built around iPhone and iPad.
- You need to fill a big room. A mini isn't made for that.

## HomePod mini 2 alternatives

- **[HomePod (2nd generation)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=HomePod+2nd+generation&tag=techtreck02-20), $349:** the better pick for a living room. It's much fuller and louder, and Apple has sold certified refurbished units for **$299**.
- **Original HomePod mini:** if you find one discounted, it's still fine for music and smart home control. It will never get Siri AI, though, so don't pay close to $149 for it.
- **Two minis vs one HomePod:** a stereo pair of minis costs $298, close to a refurbished HomePod 2. For one room, the bigger HomePod sounds better. Two minis make more sense split across two rooms.

## HomePod mini 2 FAQ

### Does the HomePod mini 2 have Siri AI?

Not yet. Apple says Siri AI is coming in a free future software update and hasn't given a date. When it arrives, it will support English in the U.S., Canada, the UK, Australia and several other countries.

### Will the original HomePod mini get Siri AI?

No. Macworld's comparison and 9to5Mac's breakdown both say the original HomePod mini stays on the older Siri. The S10 chip in the mini 2 is the difference.

### Is the HomePod mini 2 worth upgrading from the original?

Only if you want Siri AI later, better Wi-Fi range, or the Mac speaker feature. If your original mini does what you need, keep it until Siri AI ships and people have used it.
