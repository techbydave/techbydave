Oura has filed a new lawsuit to get the [Ultrahuman Ring Pro](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Ultrahuman+Ring+Pro&tag=techtreck02-20) pulled from US stores. The ring is still on sale, and no court has ordered anything. If you're choosing between the Ring Pro and an [Oura Ring](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Oura+Ring+4&tag=techtreck02-20) right now, here's what the lawsuit means for you and how the prices really compare.

*Updated November 16, 2026.*

## The quick verdict

**Wait, or buy with a long return window.** The lawsuit alone isn't a reason to panic, and patent cases move slowly. But if you're on the fence, it's a small, real reason to lean toward Oura, or to buy the Ring Pro only from a store that lets you return it.

## What happened

Oura filed a patent infringement complaint in the US District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, reported on October 22, 2026. It claims the Ring Pro infringes two Oura patents: a design patent (D804,471) and a utility patent (12,769,363). Reports say the utility patent covers a ring with a curved battery, a circuit board, magnets and sensors.

The timing stands out. One of the two patents was issued on October 20, two days before the suit was filed.

Oura wants money and a permanent injunction that would stop Ring Pro sales in the US. It also named SVTronics, a Texas company that Oura says builds the Ring Pro at a plant in Plano under Ultrahuman's direction.

Ultrahuman rejects the claims and says it will respond through the courts. Its CEO, Mohit Kumar, says the Ring Pro is a substantially redesigned ring and that no court order affects US sales.

## Why this is a rerun

This is the second time Oura has gone after Ultrahuman in the US:

- **2023 to 2025:** Oura won an International Trade Commission ruling in August 2025, and Ultrahuman's Ring Air was barred from US import.
- **February 2026:** Ultrahuman launched the redesigned Ring Pro.
- **March 2026:** US Customs and Border Protection ruled in its favor, and US pre-orders opened March 24.
- **October 2026:** Oura sued again over the Ring Pro, using different patents. Oura says the customs ruling only covered the patent from the earlier fight.

Oura has also had patent fights with Samsung and RingConn, so this is part of a pattern, not a one-off.

## Ultrahuman Ring Pro vs Oura Ring: the real prices

This is where the decision usually gets made:

- **Ultrahuman Ring Pro: $479**, with a charging case included and **no subscription**. Ultrahuman quotes up to 15 days of battery, or up to 45 days with the case.
- **Oura Ring 4 (Silver): $349**, plus a membership of **$5.99 a month or $69.99 a year**. Its charging case costs extra.
- **Oura Ring 5: from $399** in black or silver, or $499 for other finishes, with the same membership. It's about 40% smaller than the Ring 4.

Oura costs less up front, but the membership adds up. One November price comparison put two years of ownership at about $489 for the Oura Ring 4 Silver and $539 for the Ring 5, against $479 for the Ring Pro with nothing more to pay.

## Who should buy which ring

**Lean Ultrahuman Ring Pro if** you hate subscriptions, want the longest battery, and you're comfortable with some legal uncertainty. Buy from a retailer with a generous return policy.

**Lean Oura if** you want the lowest up-front price, the most established app, and no risk of your ring's maker losing a US sales fight.

**Hold off on both if** you're only mildly curious. Black Friday is November 27, so check for a price drop before you buy either one.

## Alternatives

- **RingConn:** another subscription-free ring. Oura has sued RingConn too, so check where that case stands before buying.
- **Oura Ring 4 instead of Ring 5:** the Ring 4 Silver is $50 cheaper and still a full Oura ring. The Ring 5's main gain is the smaller size.
- **Ultrahuman Ring Air:** it can't be shipped to US addresses, so it's only an option outside the US.

## Smart ring lawsuit FAQ

### Can I still buy the Ultrahuman Ring Pro in the US?

Yes. As of mid-November 2026, no court order stops US sales. The lawsuit asks for one, but that's a request, not a ruling.

### Will my Ring Pro stop working if Oura wins?

Nothing in the reporting says existing rings would stop working. A sales ban would stop new US sales. The bigger worry is future replacements, warranty support and accessories, which is speculation, not something either company has said.

### How long could the case take?

The first fight ran from 2023 until the Ring Air ban in 2025. Watch the Eastern District of Texas docket for a motion for a preliminary injunction. That would be the first sign this is moving faster.
