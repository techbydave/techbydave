The [AirPods Pro 3](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=AirPods+Pro+3&tag=techtreck02-20) are $199 at Amazon right now, $50 under Apple's $249 price. That's a fair deal and a good one for iPhone owners who need new earbuds. It's also not a once-a-year event, so skip the panic.

Early Black Friday coverage is already calling it the best Apple deal on Amazon's list. Fair enough, but the history matters. AppleInsider tracked the same $199 price back in January, when it beat Black Friday 2025 pricing by $20. MacRumors saw it return in May and again in late September, each time matching the all-time low. The Gadgeteer reported Amazon, Verizon and Costco all at $199, and said that looks like the floor until the next generation arrives. That's one outlet's read, not a promise.

**What you get:** Apple says the Pro 3 have up to twice the active noise cancellation of the Pro 2, plus heart rate sensing, Live Translation and a revised fit. They ship with five silicone tip sizes. Gizmodo puts battery at up to 10 hours in Transparency mode and around eight with ANC on. Amazon's listing also bundles up to three free months of Apple Arcade, Fitness+ and News+, according to Gizmodo.

**Who should buy:** iPhone users with old, dying or non-ANC earbuds. At $199, the price premium over cheaper AirPods is easier to justify. Costco is the other route. The Gadgeteer says its $199 offer includes AppleCare, but you need a membership.

**Who should skip:** Anyone with a working pair of [AirPods Pro 2](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=AirPods+Pro+2&tag=techtreck02-20). The upgrade is mostly noise cancellation and heart rate sensing, and people in forum comments are split on the new shape. That's anecdotal, but it's a reason to try before you commit to a swap.

**The cheaper route:** The [AirPods 4](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=AirPods+4&tag=techtreck02-20) with ANC dropped to $119 on Amazon in January, per AppleInsider. Tom's Guide has seen them as low as $99 at Walmart, though that deal may be old. They sound worse than the Pro 3, per Tom's Guide, but they keep the core Apple features and add ANC. If you mostly use earbuds for podcasts and calls, that's the smarter spend.

**Canada:** I couldn't confirm a Canadian price for this $199 deal. Check Amazon.ca and Apple's Canadian store directly before assuming the discount carries over.

**What to do:** If you need earbuds this month, buy at $199 and don't wait. If you can hold off, set a price alert. Black Friday could dip lower, and last year's sale price was $219, so $199 has already beaten it. Just don't expect a big drop below it.
