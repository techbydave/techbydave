The [Klipsch Stratos HP-2](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Klipsch+Stratos+HP-2&tag=techtreck02-20) costs $999.99 in the US and is due in December 2026. For that you get closed-back, wired-only headphones with wood earcups. Unless you're the sort of buyer who gets weak at the knees for sapele and copper trim, wait for someone to measure them first.

Klipsch showed the HP-2 and its pricier sibling, the HP-3 ($1,199.99), at CanJam Dallas on Nov. 14-15. They're the company's first new over-ear headphones in over a decade. Both should ship through Klipsch.com and authorized retailers.

**What you get**

The HP-2 pairs a 40mm bio-cellulose mid/bass driver with a separate 10mm dynamic tweeter in each cup. Klipsch pitches it for deep bass and noise isolation, even for studio monitoring. The listed specs are 32 ohms, 100 dB sensitivity, 15Hz to 18kHz (±5dB) and 380g. In the box are a 1.37m balanced cable with a 4.4mm plug, a 2.5m 3.5mm cable and a 3.5mm to 1/4" adapter.

The look is the big selling point: stained sapele wood cups in Auburn, machined aluminum yokes, copper rings, Heritage speaker grille cloth and protein-leather headbands. A walnut headband stand is a separate $149.99, also in December.

**Why I'd hold off**

There's no battery, no Bluetooth, no noise canceling. Klipsch says it will announce the final specs and price for its third, unnamed model in early 2027, so the lineup isn't finished. And one report noted that Klipsch hasn't published how it handles the usual coaxial-driver trade-offs. I haven't found any independent listening or measurement data yet, and a thousand dollars is a lot to spend on a promise and a nice finish.

Outside the US, one source lists €999 for the HP-2 in January 2027. I didn't find a confirmed Canadian price or date.

**Cheaper ways to spend it**

If you want closed-back wired hi-fi, the [Focal Azurys](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Focal+Azurys&tag=techtreck02-20) is listed at Layton Audio at C$599 on promo, down from C$699. That's Canadian retail pricing, not a US quote. If a closed-back isn't a must, the [Sennheiser HD 660S2](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Sennheiser+HD+660S2&tag=techtreck02-20) is an open-back that Audiofi lists at C$449.95 against a C$799.95 regular price. Open-backs leak sound and let noise in, so they're for quiet rooms only. Check the current price, since these promos come and go.

**Verdict: wait.** Hold off until reviews land in December. If the HP-2 measures well and you want the wood-and-copper look, it's a reasonable splurge. If you just want good wired sound, a discounted open-back or the Focal Azurys gets you most of the way for far less.
