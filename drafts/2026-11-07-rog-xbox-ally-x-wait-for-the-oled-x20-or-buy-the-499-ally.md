A $500 gap now separates the two Xbox handhelds Asus sells, and that gap is the whole buying decision.

The [[ROG Xbox Ally](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ROG+Xbox+Ally&tag=techtreck02-20) X](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ROG+Xbox+Ally+X&tag=techtreck02-20) launched on October 16, 2025 at $999.99. The smaller sibling, the ROG Xbox Ally, launched at $599.99, but one spec roundup says it has frequently dropped to $499.99 at major retailers through 2026. That roundup isn't a primary source, so check the live price before you count on it.

**What the extra money buys**

The ROG Xbox Ally X uses an AMD Ryzen AI Z2 Extreme chip, 24GB of LPDDR5X-8000 RAM and 1TB of storage. The ROG Xbox Ally has a Ryzen Z2 A, 16GB of LPDDR5-6400 and a 512GB SSD. Both have a 7-inch 1080p IPS screen at 120Hz with variable refresh rate. Asus lists the base model's battery at 60Wh, while a spec database puts the X at 80Wh. Weight goes from 670g to roughly 715g.

So the screen is the same and the guts are not. If you mostly play lighter games, or stream them, the cheaper model's pitch is easy to see. If you want to run demanding PC titles at decent frame rates, the X is the one that was built for it.

**The wrinkle: an OLED model is coming**

One source reports that Asus announced the ROG Xbox Ally X20 at Computex 2026, tied to its 20th anniversary. It's described as the first Ally with an OLED display: a 7.4-inch panel at 1080p and 120Hz with 1,400 nits peak brightness. It reportedly keeps the same chip and 24GB of RAM as the Ally X. That same source said no price or exact release date was confirmed as of August 2026, though it expected a late 2026 launch. I couldn't find a confirmed price or date since, so treat all of it as unconfirmed.

I'll also flag that Gizmodo has an article in its related-links list called "Asus Rog Xbox Ally X 17". I couldn't find details on what that is, so I'm not going to guess.

**My take**

If you were about to pay $999.99 for the ROG Xbox Ally X, hold off a bit. You'd be paying flagship money just as an OLED version of the same silicon is reportedly arriving, and handheld OLED is the upgrade people actually notice. If you want something now, the ROG Xbox Ally at or near $499.99 is the better value, since you're not paying a premium for hardware that may be superseded.

The X makes sense only if you find it discounted well below its list price and you don't care about OLED.

**What to do:** Check current prices on both models, watch Asus's announcements for a confirmed X20 price and date, and don't pay $999.99 until you know what the OLED version costs.
