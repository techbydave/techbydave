The New York Marathon winner, Andreas Almgren, wasn't wearing a Garmin or an Apple Watch Ultra. TechRadar reports he ran in a [Coros Pace 4](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Coros+Pace+4&tag=techtreck02-20), a $249 watch. So is it worth buying? For runners, yes. But if you can hold off until Black Friday on November 27, do that first.

**What you get for $249**

The Coros Pace 4 launched on November 10, 2025. It has a 1.2-inch AMOLED touchscreen, dual-frequency GPS and a redesigned heart rate sensor. It weighs 32 grams with the nylon band and 40 grams with silicone. Coros rates the battery at up to 19 days of daily use and 41 hours of GPS. OutdoorGearLab measured 40 hours with GPS on, and it says that nearly doubles what other GPS watches under $300 manage.

Outside's review is a useful reality check on the daily-use number. Its tested figure was 4 days with workouts. Plan around that, not the 19-day headline.

**What you give up**

This is a running watch, not a do-everything one. There's no ANT+ and no NFC. Music plays offline to Bluetooth headphones, with no streaming apps. Offline maps weren't there at launch. One spec site says they arrived by firmware in September 2026, starting as a public beta. The [Garmin Forerunner 165](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Garmin+Forerunner+165&tag=techtreck02-20) costs the same $249, and one review notes that Garmin Pay is still its edge. TechRadar also points to the [Suunto Run](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Suunto+Run&tag=techtreck02-20) as a similar alternative.

**The smarter move**

Coros has since launched the [Coros Pace 4 Pro](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Coros+Pace+4+Pro&tag=techtreck02-20). One reviewer lists it with a 1.47-inch 3,500-nit screen, sapphire and titanium, and up to 60 hours of GPS. That reviewer puts it about $200 above the Pace 4. Unless you race ultras, that's money for a nicer shell more than a better run.

The more interesting effect is on the cheaper watch. TechRadar says older models tend to drop when a new one arrives, especially during big sales, and it's tipping the Pace 4 as one to watch on Black Friday. No sale price is confirmed yet, so that's a bet and not a deal.

**Canada**

The Coros Pace 4 is CA$349 on Coros's Canadian store. Retailers like Altitude Sports list the same price. Some Canadian shops show it backordered or sold out, so check stock before planning around a sale.

**Verdict: wait, unless you need it now**

If your current watch still works, give it 17 days and see whether the Coros Pace 4 gets cut. If your training watch just died, $249 isn't a mistake today. Skip it if you want payments, streaming music or ANT+ sensors, and look at the Garmin Forerunner 165 instead. Before you buy, check the Coros Pace 4 price at Coros, REI and Amazon the week of November 27.
