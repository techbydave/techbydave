Arlo's new doorbells can't use Arlo's own SmartHub, which means no local storage. That's the buried detail in an otherwise small upgrade, and it's the reason I'd keep my money in my pocket for now.

**What's new**

Arlo launched the battery-powered [Arlo Video Doorbell (3rd Gen)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Arlo+Video+Doorbell+%283rd+Gen%29&tag=techtreck02-20) and the [Arlo Wired Video Doorbell (3rd Gen)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Arlo+Wired+Video+Doorbell+%283rd+Gen%29&tag=techtreck02-20). Both are $79.99 in the US, and <a href="https://androidguys.com/news/arlo-launches-third-gen-2k-video-doorbells-for-79-99/">preorders are open through Arlo and Amazon</a> with shipping expected in late November. They come in White, Black, Greystone and Sand Dune.

On paper they look a lot like the last generation. Gizmodo notes that <a href="https://gizmodo.com/arlo-video-doorbell-2k-3rd-gen-2000838862">2K video, 12X digital zoom, infrared night vision and two-way audio carry over</a>, along with a 180-degree diagonal field of view (a diagonal measurement, so don't expect a true 180 side to side). The real changes are the extra colors and a claimed battery life of six months per charge, up from four on the 2nd gen. The wired model adds Arlo Foresight, which captures footage from the seconds before motion is detected.

**The catch**

According to Arlo's own FAQ, the 3rd-gen doorbells "are not currently compatible" with SmartHub or Base Station devices. If you already own one, or hate monthly fees, that matters. Without local storage you're tied to a subscription, and <a href="https://gizmodo.com/arlo-video-doorbell-2k-3rd-gen-2000838862">plans start at $10 a month for a single camera</a>. Arlo's plan tiers (Smart Aware, Smart Secure, Ultra Secure) also split up the AI features, so not everything is included with the $79.99 hardware. Continuous 24/7 recording on the wired model needs the Ultra Secure subscription.

**The cheaper move**

The [Arlo Video Doorbell (2nd Gen)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Arlo+Video+Doorbell+%282nd+Gen%29&tag=techtreck02-20) is still sold. On October 15, Android Authority spotted it at <a href="https://www.androidauthority.com/arlo-video-doorbell-2k-deal-amazon-3723989/">$49.99 at Amazon, 38% off its $79.99 list price</a>. Walmart's listing also showed $49.99, down from $129.99. Those are past deals, so check the current price before you buy. At that level you save $30, keep SmartHub compatibility, and give up mostly a color choice and some battery life.

**Who should buy what**

- Buy the 2nd gen on sale if you want a 2K doorbell for the least money, or if you want local storage.
- Buy the Arlo Wired Video Doorbell (3rd Gen) only if you have doorbell wiring, want Foresight, and are fine paying for Arlo Secure.
- Skip the battery 3rd gen unless the Sand Dune finish or the six-month battery claim is worth $30 to you.

I couldn't find a confirmed Canadian price or release date for the 3rd gen, so I'm not guessing at one.

With Black Friday close, watch whether Arlo discounts the 2nd gen again. It has dropped under $50 before, and it will probably do it again.
